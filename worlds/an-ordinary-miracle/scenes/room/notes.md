# The Master's Room — engine 3

The second scene of «An Ordinary Miracle» (Schwartz, 1954; the game's scenario:
docs/new-games/obyknovennoe-chudo/scenario.md, §4 place 1, «Горница Хозяина»). Stage 2 of writing the game — the look:
the room on the evening before the storm, the moment of I-2 («Король»): the King has taken the best chair, the
Mistress stands by her hearth, hands on her hips, and will not give up her window; the Princess by the window watches
the young man; the Bear stands at the edge, fists clenched. A still picture (`picture`): people stand (or sit) and
breathe; the fire burns in the stove (particles `fire`, `embers`, the coals' flicker, the `hearth` light's flicker),
the two candles' flames sway and tremble, the storm clouds drift in the window and the far lightning lights them now
and then (the lib's `flash`, the sky's layer `flash`). No script, no showcase, no caption.

## The look

The yard's storybook in gouache, inside: honey-coloured hewn logs, a whitewashed stove with a painted band of blue
flowers, folk reds and blues in small things (the rug, the chest, the towel, the plates), the music box red and gold
on its shelf. The light is the room's own: the hearth (warm, strong, near the Mistress), the candles on the table
(warm, small), the window (cool storm light from the left of the middle) over a neutral `ambient` — warm without
being all orange, faces lit from the front-left.

## The frame

Horizon 0.42; the eye 1.7 m up at the front of the room (the room runs from 2 m to the back wall at 5 m). Left to
right: the stove with its open mouth (x ≈ 0.2), the Mistress before it on the rug (`hozyayka`, −1.05, 3.5), the
window with the storm and the Princess beside it (`princess`, 0.05, 4.4), the King in the best chair behind the
table (`korol`, sat on [0.75, 4.2, 0.47]), the shelf with the music box, the Bear (`medved`, 1.75, 3.35), the stair to
the loft at the right edge. The people's looks are cheated toward the viewer (points before the partner, not the
partner's head) so that their faces read in three-quarter.
