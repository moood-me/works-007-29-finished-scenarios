# cast — the people of “The Old Man and the Sea”

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}` — people_guide).

## Style

`gulf` — the engine's flat geometry (`flat`): straight edges, plain sun-faded colours, lit by the scene's light; a
fine line; a simple face (eyes, brows, nose, mouth — `<id>_mood` shows on it), so faces read in a wide shot.

## Who

- `santiago` — the old man: thin and gaunt, skin burnt dark brown, grey hair cropped close (`cropped`), a faded blue
  shirt patched many times (`patches`), old trousers, barefoot.
- `manolin` — the boy: slight, black hair, a white undershirt, dark shorts, barefoot.
- `father` — the boy's father, a fisherman of the lucky boat: thickset, a dark cap, a work shirt, dark trousers.
- `martin` — who keeps the Terrace: heavy, a white shirt, a long pale apron, dark trousers.
- `fisher_a`, `fisher_b` — young fishermen of the Terrace: bareheaded in a faded yellow shirt; in a cap and a faded
  green shirt.
- `elder` — an old fisherman of the Terrace: a straw hat, a white shirt.

## Motions (Kimodo)

The engine's `stand` and `walk`, and (loops): `hands_hips` — stands with the hands on the hips; `stand_old` — an old man
standing stooped, the knees a little bent, the shoulders rounded; `hands_behind` — stands with the hands clasped behind
the back, shifting his weight; `stand_easy` — stands, the arms hanging; `sit_table` — sitting at a table, the forearms
forward on it (`_on` a seat, `_reach` the hands onto the top); `sit_laugh` — sitting, leaning back, laughing;
`lean_counter` — stands leaning the right forearm on a counter at his right (`_reach` r onto its top);
`behind_counter` — stands behind a counter (`_reach` the hands onto its top).
