# cast — the people of “Of Mice and Men” as the engine's people

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}` — the engine's people.md). Its
data (`cast.data.json`) holds the motions, written there by `put_motion`.

## Style

`dust` — the engine's flat geometry (`flat`), its limbs heavier: straight edges, plain colours, the light and the
shadows the scene's, a fine line; the face `simple` — eyes, brows, a nose, a mouth, so that a face reads across a fire and takes `<id>_mood`.

## Who

- `george` — George Milton: small and quick, a dark face, sharp features; a short denim jacket (`denim`: to the hips), denim
  trousers, a black shapeless hat (`slouch`: its brim pushed back off the brow, the face seen under it).
- `lennie` — Lennie Small: huge, pale, shapeless of face, sloping shoulders, long heavy arms; the same denim and hat.
- `slim` — Slim, the jerkline skinner: tall and lean, a long face, black hair combed back; a dark leather vest (`vest`:
  to the hips, no sleeves) over a pale work shirt, jeans, a light Stetson (`stetson`: a wide flat brim, a tall squared
  crown, a dark band).
- `candy` — Candy, the swamper: old, stoop-shouldered, thin, white hair cropped close; a faded grey shirt, old jeans,
  no hat. (Both hands: the cast cannot take one off.)

## Motions (Kimodo)

- `pockets` — stands, the hands in the front pockets, the head up, the weight shifting a little (a loop).
- `hug_knees` — sits on the ground, the knees drawn up, the arms round them, rocking a little (a loop): a child's way of sitting.
- `solitaire` — sits (a seat: `<id>_on`), laying out cards one by one on a table before him with the right hand (a loop).
- `forearms_on_table` — sits, leaning a little forward, both forearms on a table before him, listening (a loop).
- `arms_folded` — stands, the arms crossed over the chest, the weight shifting a little (a loop).
- `shy` — stands like a child, the hands clasped before him, swaying a little side to side, the head tipped (a loop).
- `pet_dog` — sits, leaning to his left, the left hand going down to an animal on the floor beside him and back (a
  loop).
