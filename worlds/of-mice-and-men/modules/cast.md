# cast — the people of “Of Mice and Men” as the engine's people

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}` — the engine's people.md). Its
data (`cast.data.json`) holds the motions, written there by `put_motion`.

## Style

`dust` — the engine's flat geometry (`flat`): straight edges, plain colours, the light and the shadows the scene's, a
fine line; the face `simple` — eyes, brows, a nose, a mouth, so that a face reads across a fire and takes `<id>_mood`.

## Who

- `george` — George Milton: small and quick, a dark face, sharp features; a denim coat, denim trousers, a black
  shapeless hat (`slouch`: its brim pushed back off the brow, the face seen under it).
- `lennie` — Lennie Small: huge, pale, shapeless of face, sloping shoulders, long heavy arms; the same denim and hat.

## Motions (Kimodo)

- `pockets` — stands, the hands in the front pockets, the head up, the weight shifting a little (a loop).
- `sit_knees` — sits on the ground, the knees drawn up and apart, the forearms on them, rocking a little (a loop).
