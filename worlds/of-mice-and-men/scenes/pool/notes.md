# The Pool — the Salinas below the road, Thursday at sundown

The place of the prologue and the finale (scenario C1): George and Lennie camp on the sand by a deep green pool before
the ranch. Seen from the near bank looking east across the river: the pool, the far bank's willows and tules, the
valley's fields, the foothills and the Gabilan mountains; the sun is low behind the viewer (light `sun`, bearing 205,
7° up), laying the evening gold on the far bank and the mountains and on the two men's faces; the sycamore leans out
over the pool at the right.
