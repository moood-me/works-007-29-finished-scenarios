# The Barn — Sunday afternoon, the sun through the cracks

Stage 2 of the game (the look), its third scene — С4 of the scenario: the barn on Sunday afternoon (chapter 4,
4.2–4.3), before the pup is dead. Seen from the front of the aisle looking to the back doors: the stalls and the
horses down the left, the loft over everything, the pile of new hay at the back right, Slim's pups in their box by it.
The light is the afternoon sun coming in through the cracks of the right wall's boards in thin lines — over the floor,
the hay and the people — the rest of the barn brown and dim, daylight round the shut doors at the back.
