# cast — the people of “Night Flight”

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}` — people_guide). The parts'
lengths are a man's metres times `F.fit.h`.

## Style

`aero` — from the engine's `graphic` (broad shoulders over a narrow waist, square-ish — an airline poster's figures),
plain colours under the scene's light (`lit`), a fine line, a simple face (eyes, brows, nose, mouth — `<id>_mood` shows
on it), so faces read in a wide shot.

## Who

- `riviere` — Rivière, the director: past fifty, tall, upright, grey at the temples; a long dark overcoat, a dark hat.
- `robineau` — Robineau, the inspector: shorter and soft, a brown suit, dark hair, a small moustache; his folder in
  both hands.
- `roblet` — Roblet, the old mechanic: white hair, a faded blue work jacket; his cap off, held in both hands.

## Things (a look's `parts`, or `<id>_with`)

`folder` — a folder of reports held against the chest in both hands. `cap_held` — a cloth cap held before him in both
hands (its colour the look's `hat`). `moustache` — a small moustache (the hair's colour).

## Motions (Kimodo)

The engine's `stand` and `walk`, and loops: `hands_behind` — stands, the hands clasped behind the back;
`hold_chest` — stands holding something against the chest in both hands (the folder); `hold_low` — stands, the head
bowed, holding something before the belly in both hands (the cap).
