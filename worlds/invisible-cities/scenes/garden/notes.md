# The Khan's garden — the look (stage 2)

The world's first scene: the terrace over the night water where the Khan and Marco spend their nights. For now a
still picture (camera `picture`): the people stand and sit, only the small things move — the lantern's flame, the
lighthouse's flash, the boat rocking, the leaves, the stars, the ripple on the water, fireflies in the garden.

  its gap (`door_glow`, a light `door`); the gate to the shore road in the garden wall near the water;
- the low table (top 0.42) with the board and the reports, the Khan on his low seat beside it (`khan_on`), the lantern on
