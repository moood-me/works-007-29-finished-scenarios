# Ersilia — the look (stage 2)

Chapter II's city: a station in the steppe at sunset, the town already gone. The house they took apart (posts, plates,
bare trusses, a few boards), the lamp post (its lamp lit), the water tower across the line, the bench, stakes where
fences stood — and between them the threads, white, black and grey, so many that one cannot pass, but along the old
sleepers laid from the rails to the bench. The last man with his sack by the lamp post, Marco on the sleepers. A
still picture (camera `picture`): the people stand; the grass and the feather grass sway, motes hang in the low
light, the lamp flickers.

Where things are (metres; the viewer at 0, 0; the land flat):
- the railway going away to the horizon at across −1.6 (`track`, 1500 m; its sleepers drawn for the first 160 m),
  telegraph poles along it every 30 m; the water tower at −10, 28;
- the sleepers to the bench at distance 8…8.5 (across −0.4…1.8); the lamp post at 1.6, 9.6 (threads tied at 1.7, 2.6,
  3.5 m; its lamp the light `lamp`), the bench at 3.1, 8.95;
- the house from across 4.6 to 9.8, distance 11.5 to 16: `house_front` (facing the viewer), `house_side` (two copies,
  each painted from the back corner toward the viewer), `truss` (four copies), `ridge`;
- the sun has just gone down behind the viewer, on the right (the light `sun`, from 150°, 6° up): faces and the threads
  are lit warm against the violet east, the shadows long toward the horizon;
- the threads (`thread_NN`): written by `tools/ersilia-threads.js` from its list of points — each an upright sheet
  turned to run from one end to the other, painted by `lib.js` (`thread`); change the list there and run it.

The people are the world's cast: `marco`, `last_man` (`ersilian` — modules/cast.md).
