# cast — the people of «Invisible Cities»

A module for scenes with people (`"modules": ["cast"]`, a person `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}`). Style `cities`: the
engine's flat geometry (straight edges, plain quiet colours lit by the scene), a simple face. Nothing of a country or an
age: long plain clothes.

## Who

- `khan` — the Khan at seventy-seven: big, gone heavy, white cropped hair under a soft dark round cap (`round`), a white beard, a heavy plum robe
  to the floor over a pale under-robe, a walking stick leaning at his right knee (the part `stick`).
- `khan_young` — the Khan seventeen years before: strong, the beard dark, a dark wine robe, no stick.
- `marco` — Marco at thirty-seven: lean, grey at the temples, a long road-dust coat to the shin (`long_coat`), a muted
  red scarf (the one colour accent).
- `marco_young` — Marco at twenty: the same coat, darker (wet), no grey.
- `boatman` — the Khan's boatman: no age, a plain dark tunic.
- `ferrywoman` — the ferrywoman of Valdrada, about seventy: small, a long plain dress the grey-blue of lake water, a
  brown kerchief (`kerchief`) over grey hair.
- `ersilian` — the last man of Ersilia, about fifty: broad, weathered, a long brown coat, his sack on his back (`sack`).

## Parts

`kerchief` (a hat) — a kerchief over the crown and the back of the head, the face open.

`sack` — a sackcloth sack on the back between the shoulder blades. `beard` — a full beard in the hair's colour. `stick` — a walking stick, its brass knob by the right knee, its foot on the
floor beside the right foot (standing or sitting).

## Motions

`seated` — sits at rest (a chair's height; the seat through `<id>_on`), a loop. `hands_behind` — stands, the hands behind
the back, a loop. `turn_toy` — an old man turns a small thing in both hands at his chest and looks at it, a loop.
`stand` and `walk` are the engine's.
