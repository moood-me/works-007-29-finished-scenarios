# The Farm Kitchen

Stage 2 of the game (the look, docs/new-games/okean-v-konce-dorogi/scenario.md §4 «Кухня фермы»; I-3): the Hempstocks'
kitchen on a clear late morning, and three of the game's people in it, still — a breath, the fire, the steam, the
reeds in the window. No motion of people, no camera moves, no showcase (nothing happens in it yet).

- **Where things are.** Camera `picture`, horizon 0.4. The room is 6.8 m wide, 2.7 m high, its back wall at 6 m
  (the room runs on behind the viewer). Back wall (`back_wall`): limewash, copper pans on the left, the clock over the
  table's head, the deep window (x 0.2…1.8, 0.95–2.05 m: `reveal_l`, `reveal_r`, `lintel`, `sill`, `casement`,
  `sill_things`; `opening` — its unseen hole the daylight comes in at). Left wall: the dresser with plates. Right wall:
  the brick alcove of the chimney breast (x −1.85…−0.05 of its card) with the mantel; the black range in it (`range`
  front with the firebox — its coals a layer of added light, its point `range#fire`; `range_top`; `kettle`). The
  flagstone `floor`, the beamed `ceiling` (a card laid face down from the top of the back wall), `herbs` on the beam,
  the rag `rug` by the fire.
- **The table** (`table` top, `table_side`, `table_end`) along the room, left of the middle; on it the milking pail,
  the boy's porridge (jam in it: purple) with the jam jar, a loaf and a jug.
- **Outside the window**: `sky`, the field (`meadow`, a path to the pond), the duck `pond` (a mirror, rippling),
  `reeds` swaying, the far `trees`.
- **People** (the world's cast, modules/cast.md). The boy on the tall `stool` by the table (`boy_on`), looking down at
  his bowl; Lettie standing before the window, hands in her pockets, smiling at him; old Mrs Hempstock in her chair
  (`armchair`) beside the range (`gran_on`), smiling at him. Each faces between the viewer and what they look at, so
  every face reads.
- **Light.** The sun through the window (`sun`, `through` its opening: the patch on the floor by the stool, `motes` in
  it), the daylight at the window (`window_light`), the fire in the range (`fire`, key `fire`; `flames`), the steam
  from the kettle (`steam`).
