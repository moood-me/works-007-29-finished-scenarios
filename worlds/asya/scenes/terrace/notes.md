# The Gagins' Terrace (`terrace`) — engine 3

The world's first scene; it sets the look (stage 2 of the game: the mood and the style, the three main people
standing). An evening in summer on the terrace of the Gagins' white house above the Rhine, near L.; across the river,
Z. (N. N.'s inn among its houses). The sun is low behind the viewer, on the right: it lights the faces, the house, the
far bank.

**The style** — one of Gagin's études: alive and coloured in the middle, its edges a light unfinished drawing (paper,
graphite hatching). The camera is a still picture from the start, so every painting knows where it is seen across the
frame (its across over its distance) and goes over to the paper toward the sides (`lib.js`: `edge`, `unfinish`,
`hatch`; the floor also toward the frame's foot — `foot`). Green, white, blue, gold; the only red is the geranium.
