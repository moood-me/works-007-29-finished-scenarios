# cast — the people of «Asya»

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}`). Style `etude`: the engine's flat
geometry softened (limbs taper, no bars), lit by the scene, a fine line, the face `simple` (eyes, brows, nose, mouth —
`<id>_mood` works).

## Who

- `asya` — Asya, seventeen: small, slight, a swarthy round face, black hair short like a boy's in curls, a white dress.
- `gagin` — Gagin, about thirty: soft, a little heavy, a painter's olive-brown linen jacket over a white shirt.
- `nn` — N. N., twenty-five: slim, a dark green-blue frock coat (`frockcoat`), a dark cravat, light trousers.

## Things (`<id>_with`, or a look's `parts`)

## Motions

`seated` — sits upright, the hands on the lap, breathing (a loop; on a seat through `<id>_on`, the hips ~0.5 m);
`hands_behind` — stands still, the hands clasped behind the back, watching (a loop).
