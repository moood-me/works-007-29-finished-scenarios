# The Laboratory

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the laboratory (scenario C1) on an April morning — the place that sets the world's
mood (white, daylit, the spring outside, the mouse in the maze) and its three people standing as they stand in the
story. No one moves but a look; no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands at the room's open side (camera `picture`). Floor at 0, ceiling 3.0
  (`ceiling`, a card laid face down, two unlit lamp housings); the back wall at distance 5.4 (`back_wall`, the window's
  opening x −2.7…−0.9, sill 0.95, head 2.6), side walls at across ±3.0 from 1.8 to 5.4 (`left_wall`: the bookcase and
  the chart of the brain; `right_wall`: the door to the corridor at distance 3.3…4.3, the sink). Shared paint: `lib.js`.
- **Out of the window**: past 5.6 the land drops to −4 (`relief.js`): a plane tree in young leaf (`tree`, its clumps
  layers swaying in the wind), the university's old brick building (`campus`, 32 m), the April sky (`sky`, clouds
  drifting).
- **On the back wall**: the blackboard (`blackboard`: the maze's plan and the race's two curves in chalk, no words),
  the clock (`clock`: minute and second hands layers its animate turns). Under the window the bench (`counter`,
  `counter_top`) with Algernon's empty cage (`cage`) and a jar of daffodils (`flowers` — the motif of the title).
- **The maze table** in the middle (`maze_table`, its top `maze_top` with the ink-blot cards, a clipboard, a
  stopwatch); the maze on its left two thirds (`maze`, a plane with a faint mirror — the glass over it; its goal
  `maze#goal`), Algernon in a corridor (`algernon`, his head a layer that sniffs).
- **Light**: daylight — the sun in at the window (`sun`, through `window`: its patch on the floor, shafts in the haze,
  `dust` motes in them) and the window's daylight (`day`).
- **People** (modules/cast, style `lab`): Charlie behind the maze table, his hands on its edge (`charlie_reach`),
  looking down at Algernon, smiling; Miss Kinnian behind on the left, before the window, hands clasped, smiling at
  him; Dr Strauss behind on the right, hands behind his back, watching Charlie. The showcase: Charlie looks round at
  Miss Kinnian, then all three look down at Algernon, and back.
