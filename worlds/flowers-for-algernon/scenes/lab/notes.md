# The Laboratory

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the laboratory (scenario C1) on an April morning — the place that sets the world's
mood (white, daylit, the spring outside, the mouse in the maze) and its three people standing as they stand in the
story. No one moves but a look; no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands at the room's open side (camera `picture`), brought 0.8 m in and 0.25 m
  down by the state (`camera_z`, `camera_h`): faces near eye level, the table top seen low, not filling the frame. Floor at 0, ceiling 3.0
  (`ceiling`, a card laid face down, two unlit lamp housings); the back wall at distance 5.4 (`back_wall`, the window's
  opening x −2.7…−0.9, sill 0.95, head 2.6), side walls at across ±3.0 from 1.8 to 5.4 (`left_wall`: the bookcase and
  the chart of the brain; `right_wall`: the door to the corridor at distance 3.3…4.3, the sink). Shared paint: `lib.js` (the walls' paint, plain).
- **Out of the window**: past 5.6 the land drops to −4 (`relief.js`): a plane tree in young leaf (`tree`: clumps of small leaves, darker inside and
  below, each clump a layer swaying in the wind; one path a shade), the university's old brick building (`campus`, 32 m), the April sky (`sky`, clouds
  drifting).
- **On the back wall**: the blackboard (`blackboard`: the maze's plan and the race's two curves in chalk, no words),
  the clock (`clock`: minute and second hands layers its animate turns). Under the window the bench (`counter`,
  `counter_top`) with Algernon's empty cage (`cage`) and a jar of daffodils (`flowers` — the motif of the title).
- **The maze table** in the middle (`maze_table`, its top `maze_top` with the ink-blot cards, a clipboard, a
  stopwatch); the maze on its left two thirds (`maze`, a plane with a faint mirror — the glass over it; its goal
  `maze#goal`), Algernon in a corridor (`algernon`, his head a layer that sniffs).
- **Light**: daylight — the sun in at the window (`sun`, through `window`: its patch on the floor, shafts in the haze,
  `dust` motes in them) and the window's daylight (`day`).
- **People** (modules/cast, style `lab`), each standing their own way (the cast's motions): Charlie behind the maze
  table, a little stooped and shy (`shy_stoop`), his hands on its edge (`charlie_reach`), looking down at Algernon,
  smiling; Miss Kinnian behind on the left, before the window, upright, her exercise book held to her chest
  (`notebook_held`), smiling at him; Dr Strauss behind on the right, turned to Charlie, his chin in his hand, thinking
  (`chin_in_hand`; his coat's skirt hangs from the waist — hung from the shoulders it flared into a bell over the
  arms). The showcase: over Charlie's head Miss Kinnian and Dr Strauss look at each other, then all three look down
  at Algernon.
