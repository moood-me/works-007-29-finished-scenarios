# The Shed

Stage 2 of the game (the look): Niggle's shed (С2 of the scenario) on a late autumn afternoon — the place that sets the
world's mood (old boards, the low sun in at the door, the lantern, the great unfinished canvas) and its three people
standing as they stand in the story. No one moves but a look; no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands at the shed's open side (camera `picture`), like a stage. Floor at 0, roof
  boards at 4.2 (`ceiling`, a card laid face down from the back wall); the back wall at distance 5.6 (`back_wall`, the
  doorway x 1.0…2.6, 2.3 high; the door's leaf `door` swung in), side walls at across ±3.2 from 1.05 to 5.6
  (`left_wall`: a shelf of jars, an old coat, old pictures leaning; `right_wall`: a window x −0.6…0.6 of it, Niggle's
  leaf sketches pinned round it, the card of the journey on its nail by the bag). Boards and leaves: `lib.js`.
- **The canvas** (`canvas`, 5.15, turned a little to us): the Tree branching again and again, leaves at every twig,
  those round the finished one washed in, grey patches, the forest begun, the Mountains with snow at the top right;
  the finished leaf at the crown's right edge (`canvas#leaf`), its dew drop's glint a flickering layer (`canvas#dew`);
  old pictures stitched on at the edges (harbour, hill, house).
- **Before it** the platform (`platform` — its front and steps; `platform_top` — the plane Niggle stands on, 0.9 up),
  the lantern on its cord (`lantern`, swaying a very little; light `lamp`, key `lamp`), the paint table, the empty
  travelling bag in the near right corner (`bag`).
- **Outside** (through the doorway and the window): Niggle's thistles by the door (`thistles`, copies, in the wind),
  the yard with the path to the gate, the fence, Parish's cabbages, Parish's cottage (`house`, 12.2: its bedroom window
  `house#window`, the room behind it `house_room`, a small lamp on the sill — light `parish_lamp`), the old apple tree
  in the side window, the sky (`sky`, repainted by `dusk` in steps).
- **Light**: the low sun from beyond the cottage (`sun`) comes in only through the doorway — its beam on the floor, the
  dust in it (`motes`), Parish's shadow; the daylight of the doorway and the window (`door_day`, `window_day`); the
  lantern. `dusk` 0…1 — the afternoon toward sunset (a look).
- **People** (modules/cast, style `niggle`): Niggle on the platform, brush in hand, turned to us, looking aside at
  nothing — not at Parish (`niggle_look` a point by the door), tired; Parish in the doorway, standing on his stick, not
  coming in, looking at Niggle; Mrs Parish at her window across the yard, by the lamp, looking at the shed.
- **Showcase**: the afternoon; Niggle's eyes go to his leaf, Parish's to the thistles at his feet (never the canvas);
  the light goes to sunset; back.
