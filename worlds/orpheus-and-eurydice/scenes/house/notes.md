# The House Below — notes

Chapter III's place (stage 2, the look): the house under the earth — a hall of grey stone seen from its open side, an
old king's hall with a round hearth in the middle of its floor, a doorway in the back wall open onto the pale meadow,
two shades standing in it. Hades sits in his heavy chair by the fire; Orpheus stands across the fire from him by the
doorway, his lyre held before him; Eurydice stands before the pomegranate tree, looking down; Persephone stands by the
tree looking at her. One feeling: a judgement waited for. Camera `picture`. Nobody moves but for breathing, the fire,
its embers and its light.

## How it is built

- Walls: `back_wall` (10.6 m, facing 180) and `side_wall` (two copies, facing 90 and 270, at ±4.6 m) — one masonry
  (`lib.wall`: courses of blocks to about 3 m, rock above to 8 m). The doorway is cut out of the back wall's painting
  (`back_wall#door`); `beyond` (13.5 m, glows) is the meadow seen through it; the light `door` stands just behind the
  wall so it lights the room and the shades, not the wall's face.
- `floor` — flags, 2 … 13.5 m (out under the doorway). `sky` — only the dark over the walls, for very tall screens.
- The hearth: `hearth` (a low stone ring at (−1.2, 6.2), `hearth#fire`), `coals` (glow, flickering layer), `fire`
  and `embers` (particles), the light `hearth_fire` (2.0, reach 8) — the warm light on every face.
- People (`modules/cast`): `hades` seated (`seated`) at (−2.6, 7.4), `hades_on` the seat 0.48 m, turned 130 to the
  fire and the hall, looking into the fire; `throne` — a card 0.3 m behind him turned as he is (move it with him);
  `orpheus` at (0.3, 8.6) turned 247, looking at Hades, his hands on `lyre#arm` and `lyre#strings` (the `lyre` card —
  the pier's — before his chest, facing 215 so it reads); `eurydice` at (1.9, 7.5) turned 215, looking at the floor
  before her (`sad`); `persephone` at (3.1, 8.8) by the `tree`, looking at Eurydice; `shade_a`, `shade_b` in the
  doorway, looking at Orpheus. Those at the back face the viewer, those at the front show three-quarters: a circle
  closed toward the viewer showed backs.
- The pomegranates are dusky gold with a brown blush: the world's one red is Eurydice's ribbon.

## The showcase

`the hall` (10 s) → `hades` → `orpheus` → `persephone` → `eurydice` (each medium) → `the door` (the scene's camera).
