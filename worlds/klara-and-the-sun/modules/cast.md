# cast — the people of “Klara and the Sun”

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}` — people_guide).

## Style

`sun` — made from `soft` (rounded shapes, tinted lines, toned colours) with a smaller head, so the grown-ups read as
grown-ups; a simple face (eyes, brows, nose, mouth — `<id>_mood` shows on it), so faces read across a room.

## Who

- `klara` — Klara, the Artificial Friend: small and slight, a dark bob, porcelain-pale, a plain slate-blue shift dress to the knee over dark tights (garment `shift`).
- `josie` — Josie, fourteen and ill: thin, pale, long honey-brown hair, a big oatmeal cardigan over a pale-blue top,
  grey leggings, socks; her sketchbook with her (part `sketchbook`, point `josie#sketchbook`).
- `mother` — the Mother (Chrissie): tall, upright, dark hair in a bun, a short straight charcoal work jacket (garment `blazer`), dark trousers.
- `rick` — Rick, the boy next door, fourteen, not lifted: tousled dark hair, an old olive canvas jacket to the hips
  (garment `canvas`) over a pale shirt, brown corduroys, work boots; his bird with him (part `bird`, point `rick#bird`).
- `manager` — the Manager of the shop: middle-aged, a little stout, short greying hair, a plain plum-grey dress to below the knee, long-sleeved (garment `frock`).

## Parts

- `sketchbook` — a pad lying level in the lap under the hands; its point `#sketchbook`.
- `bird` — Rick's bird: a small grey-blue drone made like a bird (a round body, a head with one glowing amber eye, flat
  wings folded, a short tail), held between the hands, its head away from him; its point `#bird`.

## Motions (Kimodo)

The engine's `stand` and `walk`, and: `hands_clasped` — stands upright and still, hands clasped before her (a loop);
`hand_to_chin` — stands upright, one arm across the waist holding the other elbow, that hand at the chin, watching (a loop); `drawing` — sits on a bed's
edge, a sketchbook on the knees, drawing with the right hand (a loop; seated — `<id>_on` a seat); `still` — stands upright and
still, arms hanging at the sides (a loop: under a reach — a palm on a glass); `arms_folded` — stands still, both feet flat,
arms folded low across the waist, waiting (a loop); `holding` — stands still, a small thing held in both hands before
him at the waist, turning it a little in the fingers (a loop: Rick with his bird — his look on `rick#bird`).
