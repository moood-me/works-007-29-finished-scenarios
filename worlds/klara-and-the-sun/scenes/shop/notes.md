# The Shop Window

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the shop of Artificial Friends (scenario C1) on a morning, from the street — the
prologue's moment: Josie outside, her palm on the glass; Klara inside on the window's floor, her palm against Josie's
through the glass, in the Sun; the Manager inside by the counter, watching. The city's light to the room's: a slate-
green shop front between cream and ochre houses, the Sun from the right lying in squares on the window's floor. No
one moves but a look and a face; no camera moves (the start brought 1.6 m in: `camera_z`); the light breathes (a
cloud), dust in the sun within.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands on the pavement (camera `picture`). The street front is one card
  (`front`, distance 6.0, from across −18 to 18, 12.4 high): the shop between pilasters (across −3.15…3.15) — fascia

  (across 1.7…2.6; `door` paints it) —, three storeys of stucco above, houses either side. The window: its glass
  (`pane`, `glass`: the street faintly mirrored in it) and its frame (`window`, 2 cm before it: two mullions at across
  −1.4 and 0.3, a transom at 2.0 — six panes; it casts the Sun's shadow, the squares). `pavement` (a plane before it),
  `lamp_post` (a street lamp at the right edge, its shadow along the pavement), `sky` (seen only above the roofs).
- **The shop within** (all `inside`): the window's floor (`display`, a plane at 0.45 that people stand on, 6.0…7.44),

- **The showcase**: the palm; the Manager looks at the girl, then back at Klara (tired); a cloud crosses the Sun;

What is not right yet / what the engine lacks:
- Josie, looking up at Klara through the glass, turns her head away from the viewer: at the start her face is seen
  from behind (her cheek); it is seen whole only when she looks round at the street. The glass between them puts
  Klara always farther from the viewer than Josie, and a palm on it puts them within an arm's reach — so a look at
  Klara is a look away.
- The two palms are given one point on the glass (2 cm either side), but the hands drawn stop a hand's width apart:
  the reach seems not to bring the figure's drawn hand (the style's proportions) to the point. Not tuned around.
- The glass shows only by the faint street in it (by day a glass mirrors hardly at all): the palms read as two
  hands reaching to each other more than as two palms on a pane.
