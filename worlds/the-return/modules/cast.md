# cast — the people of «The Return» as the engine's people

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}` — people_guide). The parts' lengths
are a man's metres, multiplied by `F.fit.h` (the height as a share of 1.76 m).

## Style

`gouache` — made from the engine's `soft`, with natural proportions (a man's head, neck and legs), round, a tinted painted
edge, flat colours lit by the scene's own light (`paint: lit`), the face `simple` (eyes, brows, a nose, a mouth; it
takes `<id>_mood`). Like the whole world: gouache on rough paper, sand, ochre, grey-green cloth.

## Who

- `ivanov` — Captain Alexei Alexeyevich Ivanov, 36: lean, upright; a greatcoat (`greatcoat`) to mid-shin, a side cap
  (`pilotka`), boots, the kit bag on his back (`kitbag`), the belt over the coat (`belt`).
- `lyuba` — Lyubov Vasilyevna, his wife, 33: slight; a faded blue dress, a grey linen apron (`apron`), a white kerchief
  (`kerchief`).
- `petrushka` — Petrushka, the son, 11: thin; a cut-down man's shirt, patched trousers, barefoot.

## Motions

The engine's `stand` and `walk`; the world's: `hands_behind` (stands still, the hands clasped behind the back, looking
far ahead; a loop).
