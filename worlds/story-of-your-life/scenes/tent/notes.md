# The Tent at the Looking Glass

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the tent at the looking glass (scenario §4, chapter I) on a summer morning — the
place that sets the world's mood (olive army canvas, the white light of the glass, a month of semagrams pinned up)
and its three people standing as they stand in the story. No one moves but a look; no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands at the tent's open end (camera `picture`, horizon 0.36). The tent: across
  −4.2…4.2, the back wall at distance 7.0, eaves 2.3 m, the ridge 4.4 m (`left_wall`, `right_wall` — `facing` 90/270;
  `roof_left`, `roof_right` — cards leaned in, `tilt` −65.5; all canvas, `translucent`). Shared paint: `lib.js`
  (`canvas`, `semagram`, `sheet`, `heptapod`).
- **The looking glass**: a half circle 3 m high cut out of `back_wall` (its middle at across −0.8), its grey rim and
  sill; on it a semagram (`glass_sign`, point `glass_sign#sign`). Beyond it the heptapods' white room: `room_back`
  (glows), `room_floor`, a drifting haze (`room_mist`), Flapper (`flapper`, across −2.0, distance 9.2) and Raspberry
  (`raspberry`, farther, behind the haze). Each heptapod: back limbs painted, the barrel a layer swaying (`body`), the
  front limbs (`front`), one limb lifted off the floor, drifting slowly (`arm`).
- **In the tent**: the whiteboard on its stand (left), the video camera on its tripod turned to the glass, the
  folding table (`table`, `table_top`) with papers, the laptop (its screen's glow), the thermos and cups; the plywood
  floor with cables coming in at the doorway.
- **The doorway** in the right wall (distance 4.6…5.8, `doorway` unseen): the morning sun comes in through it
  (`sun`, `through`) — a warm patch on the floor, dust in it; the meadow, the far trees, the sky beyond (`meadow`,
  `treeline`, `sky`).
- **Light**: the glass's cool white (`glass`, and `room` in the white room) against the sun's warm patch; ambient
  high — faces read.
- **People** (modules/cast, style `glass`): Louise before the glass, a marker in her hand, looking at Gary, smiling;
  Gary sitting on the table's front edge (`gary_on`, motion `seated`), holding out a cup of coffee to her
  (`gary_reach`); Colonel Weber near the doorway, hands behind his back, watching her. The showcase: Louise turns to
  Flapper, Gary to the sign, Weber to the glass; then back.
