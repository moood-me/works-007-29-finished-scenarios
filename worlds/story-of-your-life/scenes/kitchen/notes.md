## What is not right yet

- Louise's short grey hair (the shapes' `short`) reads as a close cap rather than hair.
- Louise is small in the frame (seated, 3.55 m off) — the price of keeping her face to us while she looks at her
  daughter; a nearer Louise would show us the back of her head.
- The stepladder is a flat card seen from the front: no depth to its A-frame.
- The steam over the mug is faint; the car's exhaust comes out below the window's sill line and is hardly seen.
- The engine's known limits, met or avoided here: a coil of rope is a part on her (fine), but there is still no rope
  between two people; no fabric hangs at a back (the jacket is painted on the wall, not worn); the seated clip
  from the cast (`seated`) puts the hands to the sides, so Louise sits by her own `seated_mug`, her hands held to the
  mug by `reach`.
