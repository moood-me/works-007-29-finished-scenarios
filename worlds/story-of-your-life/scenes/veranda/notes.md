# The Veranda at Night

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the back of the house (scenario §4, «Веранда») on the night of the prologue — the
third place of the world, Louise at thirty-six and Gary under the full moon, dancing slowly on the deck a minute
before his question. Moonlight and the house's warm lights. No one moves but the sway of the dance, a look and a
face; no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands on the deck's front (camera `picture`, horizon 0.5); the deck (`deck`, a
  `walk` plane at 0.5 m, across −3.6…2.4, distance 1.6…5.8; its front with the lattice `deck_front`, below the
  frame). The house's back wall at distance 5.8 (`house`, its left corner at across −2.4, the eave at 3.35 m; the
  roof's slope `roof`, a card leaned back 55°).
- **The kitchen window** (across −1.7…−0.4, 1.5…2.65 m up, cut out of `house`): its trim (`window_frame`), the café
  curtains inside (`curtains`, translucent, two layers stirring), the lamp over the table lit (`kitchen_lamp`, its
  layer `lamp` swaying a hair), and the kitchen's far wall (`kitchen`): the sage cupboard, the spice shelf, the
  stepladder folded against the wall — the same as in `kitchen` (scene 2).
- **The back door** (across 1.1…2.0) with its four lit panes (`door_glass`, glows) and the porch light beside it
  (`porch_lamp`, its glow `bulb`).
- **The side railing** along the deck's right edge (`railing`, `facing` 270; its top `rail_cap`), on it the candle in a
  jar and the two wine glasses (`glasses`: the layer `flame` leaning and stretching, its light `flame`).
- **The yard**: the lawn with the worn path to the left (`lawn`), the old maple (`maple`, three crown layers in the
  wind), the back fence (`fence`), the neighbours' trees and a lit window beyond (`treeline`), fireflies over the grass
  (`fireflies`). The sky (`moon_sky`): stars and the full moon high on the left (its point `disc`).
- **Light**: the moon (`moon`, from `moon_sky#disc`) behind the house's corner, the blue night all round (ambient
  0.55); warm on the faces from the porch light (`porch`), the candle (`candle`) and the kitchen window
  (`kitchen_light`, `window_spill`).
- **People** (modules/cast, style `glass`): Gary (`gary`) and Louise (`louise`), a step apart, in profile to each other,
  both doing `sway` (a new motion of the cast); the hold is their reaches — his right hand on her back, his left and
  her right hand meeting between them, her left hand on his right shoulder. The staging keeps her face to us: she
  stands nearer to us on the right, and her look past him into the yard comes to us in three quarters; his face comes
  to us when he looks out over her shoulder.
- **The showcase**: she looks past him, thinking, he at her, smiling; he looks out over her shoulder, she up at him,
  smiling; he looks back at her, grave — the question coming — and her look goes past him again, sad; then each to
  the other, smiling.

## What is not right yet

- In their hold he is mostly seen from behind (a head with no face) whenever he looks at her — the price of her face
  to us; the moon is behind them, so a look at it turns both faces away.
- The hold is reaches over a sway made for one: Kimodo moves one person (a slow dance asked of it gave wandering hands
  and hands on his own hips), so the two do not truly lean into each other; their sways are the same motion, in step.
- The deck is wide and empty in the frame's lower part.
- The engine's known limits, met or avoided here: no rope between two people (not needed here); no fabric hanging at
  a back; the cast's `seated` puts the hands to the sides (no one sits here); a sitting skirt is a lump (Louise is in
  trousers); fire particles are balls — the candle's flame is a painted layer, not particles.
