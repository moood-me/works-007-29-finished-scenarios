# cast — the people of “The Turn of the Screw”

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}` — people_guide). The parts'
lengths are a man's metres times `F.fit.h`.

## Style

`bly` — the engine's flat geometry (`flat`): straight edges, plain quiet colours, lit by the scene's light; a fine
line; the world's own face `bly` — the engine's simple face drawn bolder (large dark eyes, firm brows, a nose, a rose
mouth, a flush on the cheeks; `<id>_mood` shows on it), so faces read in a wide shot.

## Who

- `governess` — the governess: twenty, slight and upright, brown hair in a bun, a plain slate-grey gown to the ground,
  a white collar.
- `flora` — Flora, eight: fair hair loose to the shoulders, a white frock to the knees, a pale blue sash, white
  stockings.
- `miles` — Miles, ten: dark chestnut hair cut short, a navy Eton jacket, a white collar, grey knee breeches.

## Things (a look's `parts`, `garment`)

Garments: `gown` (to the ground, long sleeves), `frock` (to the knees, long sleeves), `eton` (a boy's short jacket to
the waist). Parts: `collar` — a white collar round the neck; `sash` — a sash round the waist in the look's `accent`.

## Motions (Kimodo)

The engine's `stand` and `walk`, and: `hands_behind` — stands still with the hands clasped behind the back, looking
ahead (a loop).
