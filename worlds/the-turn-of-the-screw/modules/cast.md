# cast — the people of “The Turn of the Screw”

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}` — people_guide). The parts'
lengths are a man's metres times `F.fit.h`.

## Style

`bly` — the engine's flat geometry (`flat`), softened: plain quiet colours lit by the scene's light, a fine line;
limbs that taper as a body's do (`arms`/`legs` `bly`: the wrist, a small hand, the calf, a slender ankle, a shoe),
garments hanging in curves (not `straight`), a head a little smaller; the world's own face `bly` — the engine's simple
face a little stronger (dark almond eyes, fine brows, a nose, a rose mouth, the least warmth on the cheeks;
`<id>_mood` shows on it), to read in a wide shot and stay quiet close.

## Who

- `governess` — the governess: twenty, slight and upright, brown hair in a bun, a plain slate-grey gown to the ground,
  a white collar.
- `flora` — Flora, eight: fair hair loose to the shoulders, a white frock to the knees, a pale blue sash, white
  stockings.
- `miles` — Miles, ten: dark chestnut hair cut short, a navy Eton jacket, a white collar, grey knee breeches.
- `grose` — Mrs Grose, the housekeeper: past fifty, short and stout, greying hair under a white mob cap, a plum-brown
  dress to the ground, a white apron.
- `jessel` — Miss Jessel, the governess before: tall, pale, dark hair loose down her back, a black gown to the ground.

## Things (a look's `parts`, `garment`)

Garments: `gown` (to the ground, long sleeves), `frock` (to the knees, long sleeves), `eton` (a boy's short jacket to
the hips), `housedress` (a dress to the ground and a white apron from the waist to the shin hanging as cloth over the
skirt — laid where the skirt hangs from the trunk's hips section). Hats: `mobcap` — a white linen cap with a frill.
Parts: `collar` — a white collar round the neck; `sash` — a sash round the waist in the look's `accent`; `fern` — a
handful of russet bracken held before one in both hands (`<id>_with: ["fern"]`).

## Motions (Kimodo)

The engine's `stand` and `walk`, and (loops, standing still): `hands_behind` — the hands clasped behind the back,
looking ahead; `hands_clasped` — the hands clasped low before the waist; `stand_still` — the arms hanging, the head a
little forward, tired; `hand_at_waist` — upright, the right hand across the waist; `hold_before` — a child's both
hands before her at the waist, holding something small.
