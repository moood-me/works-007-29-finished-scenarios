# Longbourn, the drawing room

(scenario: places — Longbourn, the drawing room: sofa, a window to the garden, Jane's embroidery frame), an early
autumn evening, the window still light and the candles lit early. It shows the family's provincial home beside
Pemberley's grandeur, and three of the sisters: Jane, Elizabeth, Lydia. People stand or sit; only the light lives —
