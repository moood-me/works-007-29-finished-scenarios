# cast — the people of «Pride and Prejudice»

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}` — people_guide). In the
scene's `modules`. The parts' lengths are a man's metres times `F.fit.h`, their thicknesses times `F.k`.

## Style

`austen` — slender, long-legged, small-headed figures (from `soft`): colours with their shade (`paint: toned`), a fine
line a darker shade of what it edges (`tinted`) — a light wash and a pen line, as Hugh Thomson drew the novel. Faces
`simple` (eyes, brows, a mouth: `<id>_mood` shows on them). The light is the scene's.

## Who

- `elizabeth` — Elizabeth Bennet, twenty: not tall, light; dark hair up in a knot, curls at the temples (`curls`),
  bareheaded; a pale high-waisted gown (`gown`), long sleeves, a sage-green sash under the bust (`sash`, the look's
  `accent`).
- `darcy` — Fitzwilliam Darcy, twenty-eight: tall, straight, dark-haired; a dark blue coat to mid-thigh
  (`tailcoat`), a white cravat (`cravat`), fawn breeches, black boots.

The world's three rare colours — Lydia's scarlet ribbon, Charlotte's grey gown, the blue seal of Darcy's letter — are
kept for those three; nobody else wears them.

## Motions

The engine's `stand` and `walk`; and `hands_behind` (stands with the hands clasped behind the back, a loop — Darcy's
way of standing; taken from Kimodo's motion of the same name in pavel/scarlet-sails).
