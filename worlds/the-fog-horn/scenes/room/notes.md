# The Room

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the keepers' room under the lantern, at sunset (chapter 1: the voice, supper, the
second cup) — the place, its light and its two people standing. No motion of people but a look, no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands at the open side of a round room (camera `picture`): its middle at distance
  3.6, radius 3.1, floor at 0, ceiling at 2.9. The wall is ten flat pieces (`wall`, copies, each a twentieth of a turn,
  facing the middle) and the back piece with the window's opening (`window_wall`); the opening goes back through 0.7 m
  of stone (`reveal` ×2, `sill`, `soffit`) to the window itself (`window`, six panes; its rectangle is where the sun
  and the sky come in: lights `sun` "through" it and `sky_in` "daylight" of it). The ceiling is a card laid face down
  (`tilt` −90, its foot at the back); the open hatch in it (`hatch`) over the ladder glows with `beacon`.
- **Out of the window**, 14 m down (the sea plane at −14; `relief.js` drops the land under it past the wall): the sun
  going down just in the window, the far shore's end with Nora's house (`far_shore#window`, key `nora`), the fog bank
  coming on the right (tomorrow's).
- **Round the room**: left, McDunn's stove (`stove`, its fire `stove_fire`, key `stove`; the kettle's steam from
  `stove#spout`); the fog horn's lever with the winter list beside it (`lever#handle`, `lever#list`); the round table
  in the middle with the oil lamp (`table_lamp`, key `lamp`) and the two mugs (`table_things#cup`,
  `table_things#second_cup`); right, the ladder to the hatch and the two bunks.
- **People.** McDunn by the lever, hands behind his back, looking at Johnny; Johnny on the right, the unlit hand
  lantern in his hand (the same as on the rock), looking down at the second cup. Cast: modules/cast.
- **Keys.** `night` (the sunset → the blue night in the window: the sky's layers cross-fade, the light's look; the sun's
