# The Rock

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the rock of the lighthouse at the last clear evening before the fog — the place, its
light and its two people standing. No motion of people, no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands on the rock (camera `picture`). The rock is the land (`relief.js`: a long
  back of stone to the tower at across 15, distance 40, falling into the sea at -1.2 on its sides) painted by the
  draped plane `rock` (wet and weedy where it goes under the water — the painting reads the land's height). The sea is
  one mirror plane. The far shore (Nora's) is a card at 900 m on the left, her window its glow `window`; the fog bank
  lies on the horizon on the right; a mist band over the water.
- **The tower** is a form (`revolve`); on it: the steps, the room's window, the gallery, the lamp behind the glass
  (`lantern_glass`, glowing: its glass grey by day, warm when the lamp is lit), the lantern room's frame and roof, the
  beam (`beam`: two additive layers turned about the lamp by its animate — a turn every 14 s, foreshortened, brightest
  coming toward the viewer; stronger by night).
- **People.** Johnny (left, the unlit lantern in his right hand, looking down at the water — his face three-quarters to the viewer; a look at the far shore itself would turn him away) and McDunn (hands behind
  his back, looking at Johnny). Cast: modules/cast.
- **Keys.** `night` (the evening → the night: the sky's layers cross-fade, the light's look), `lamp`, `nora` (her
  window), `lantern` (the hand lantern's light; `johnny_with` to `["lantern_lit"]` with it).
- **The surf** bursts on the rock in the water at across -7.4 by its own animate, irregularly.
