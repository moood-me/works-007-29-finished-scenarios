# The Lantern Room

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the top of the tower at night — the lantern room and the gallery round it (С2 of the
scenario: 1.4 the clear night and Nora's window, 2.1–2.5 the fog, the voice, him). The place, its light, its two people
standing, and him rising out of the sea. No motion of people but a look, no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands in the air beyond the gallery (camera `picture`), the eye level with the
  gallery's people, 18.7 m over the sea (`relief.js`: the land far under the water; 0 only at the eye). Everything of
  the tower stands on the unseen `top` (its middle at the gallery's floor, across −3.6, distance 7.6) in the tower's own
  metres: the tower under the gallery (`tower`, a form: slab, corbels, stone down to the sea), the gallery's floor
  (`deck`, a plane people walk on), the railing (`railing`, twenty copies round it), the lantern room — ten glazed
  sides (`facets`, nine copies; the tenth, `door_facet`, has the doorway, its iron `door` open onto the gallery), the
  lens in the middle (`lens`, glows), the domed roof (`roof`, a form) and its `finial`.
- **The lamp.** `lens#lamp` is the lamp's light round about (it lights the two men); `lens#beam_a`, `lens#beam_b` are
  the two beams — the engine's beams (lights with `cone`), turned by the keys `beam`, `beam_b`, which `script.js` moves
  on, a turn every 14 s (as the beam seen from the rock). In the fog's haze the cones are seen in the air. The lens's
  glow flares as a beam comes round toward the viewer; his eyes flare as it comes round to him (bearing 24).
- **Out at sea**: the moon (sky), the far shore with Nora's window (`far_shore#window`, key `nora`; the same painting
  as from the rock), the white wall of the fog on the horizon, two drifting mist bands (thicker with `fog`).
- **Him** (`beast`, across 15, distance 50, at the sea's face): a card of layers — the hump of his back, the neck
  (swaying about its foot under the water), the head (following the neck's top, looking about) with two eyes (glows
  `eye_r`, `eye_l`); `beast` 0…1 brings him up out of the sea (the layers moved, nothing repainted; under the water the
  sea hides them); `beast#head` is where the head is (the men look there). `wake` — the rings on the water about him.
- **People.** McDunn on the gallery before the door, Johnny at the railing with McDunn's hand lantern (lit by default). Cast:
  modules/cast.
- **Keys.** `fog` (the clear night → the fog: the light's look `fog`, the sky's layers cross-faded, the moon's light
  down), `lamp`, `beast`, `nora`, `lantern` (as in the other scenes); `beam`, `beam_b` are the script's.
