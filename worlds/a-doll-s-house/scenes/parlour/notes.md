# The Helmers' Parlour

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the Helmers' flat as a doll's house with its front taken away, on Christmas Eve —
the place, its light and three of its people standing. No motion of people but a look, no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** Camera `picture`, horizon 0.55 (the frame's height is its focal length: at distance d the
  frame shows d metres up and down), the start 0.6 m in (`camera_z`): the cabinet's frame just round the edges, the
  room and its people as large as it lets them be. The cabinet's front (`front`, at 4.6) frames the room's opening, 6 m by 3 m: the
  cut plaster of the walls' ends and of the floor above, green-black lacquer and gilt round it, a cornice; its base
  before the opening is the plane `ledge`. The room runs back to the back wall at 8.4 (floor 0, ceiling 3, walls at
  across ±3; the ceiling a card laid face down, `tilt` −90, its foot at the back).
- **The back wall**: the window to the street on the left (across −1.75…−0.55, 0.8–2.6 m; `window` is its casement,
  its rectangle the light `window_in`), the open doorway to the hall on the right (0.3…1.5, 2.4 m), red portieres;
  the piano before its left end. **Left wall** (`left_wall`, +x running back): the nursery door near the front, the
  tiled stove (`stove`: its fire `stove#fire`, key `stove`, the clock on top `stove#clock`) and the armchair before it.
  **Right wall** (+x running to the front): the study door (`right_wall#study`); the tree in the back right corner
  (`tree`; its candles' flames `tree_candles`, key `candles`). The round table with the oil lamp (`table_lamp`, key
  `lamp`) and the bag of macaroons (`table_things#macaroons`).
- **The hall** behind the doorway (8.4…10.8): its far wall `hall_back` with the front door and the locked letter box
  with its little glass window (`hall_back#letterbox`), the coat pegs, the wall lamp (`hall_back#sconce`, light
  `hall`).
- **Outside the window** the land drops 3.2 m to the street (`relief.js`): the gas lamp's head (`gas_lamp`), the
  houses across at 18 m with their windows lit in the evening, snow falling (`snowfall`, only out there).
- **People** (cast: modules/cast — dolls of the `doll` style), staged so every face is turned to the open front:
  Nora downstage in the middle, the parcel in her hands, smiling out; Torvald upstage right between the doorway and
  the tree, hands behind his back, smiling at her; Krogstad in the hall by the letter box, in his coat and hat,
  looking at her. (Two people on one depth looking at each other show the viewer their profiles: keep the one looked
  at nearer the front.)
- **Light.** The room's own lamps (the table's `lamp`, the tree's `tree`, the stove, the hall's wall lamp), the evening
  at the window (`window_in`), the gas lamp outside (`street`) — and `room`: the lamp of the room the doll's house
  stands in, before it and above (across 0.6, distance 1.2, 3.6 m up), unseen, its light on the people's faces through
  the open front.
- **Keys.** `day` (the evening ↔ a snowy day: the sky's layers, the light's look, the windows across and the gas
  lamp), `lamp`, `candles`, `stove`.
