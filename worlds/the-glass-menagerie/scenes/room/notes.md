# The Flat

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the Wingfields' living room on an evening — the place that sets the world's mood
(lamplight, the glass, the alley's neon through the window) and its three people standing as they stand in the
story. No one moves but a look; no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands at the room's open side (camera `picture`), like a stage. Floor at 0,

- **Through the arch**: the portieres (`portiere`), the dining room's wall and sideboard (7.6), the table laid with a
