# The Flat

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the Wingfields' living room on an evening, the place that sets the world's mood —
lamplight, the glass, the alley's neon — and its three people standing as they stand in the story. No one moves but a
look; no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands at the room's open side (camera `picture`), like a stage. Floor at 0,
  ceiling 2.8; the back wall at distance 5.4 (`back_wall`, its two openings: the window x −2.05…−0.95, the arch
  x 1.05…2.45), side walls at across ±2.9 (`left_wall`, `right_wall`: the door to the landing at distance 5.5, Tom's
  overcoat on the hook at 3.65). Shared wallpaper: `lib.js`.
- **Out of the window**: the fire escape's railing (6.1), the land dropping to −9 past 6.8, left of the dining room (`relief.js`), the dance
  hall's building across the alley (`facade`, 14 m) with the Paradise's neon sign on its roof (`sign`, glows; its tubes
  a flickering layer), the night sky (`sky`).
- **Through the arch**: the portieres (`portiere`), the dining room's wall and sideboard, the table laid with a white
  cloth, the lamp hanging over it (`dining_lamp`, key `dining`).
- **In the room**: the father's photograph over the daybed (`photo#face`), the daybed (`sofa`), the victrola in the
  left corner, the floor lamp (`floor_lamp`, key `lamp` — the main light), the glass menagerie on its whatnot
  (`shelf`; the unicorn `shelf#unicorn`; the glints are a layer the element's shader makes shine, uniform `shine`),
  the typewriter table by the door with a green-shaded lamp (`desk`, key `desk`).
- **Light**: warm lamps inside; the neon's pink and green and the night's blue come in at the window (point lights
  just outside it, `neon_pink`, `neon_green` by `neon`, `night`).
- **People** (modules/cast, style `memory`): Laura at the shelf, her right hand on the unicorn, looking at it; Amanda
  before the daybed, hands clasped, watching Laura; Tom by the door, arms folded, looking out at the viewer — the
  narrator.
