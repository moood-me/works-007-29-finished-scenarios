# cast — the people of “No One Writes to the Colonel”

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}` — people_guide). The parts'
lengths are a man's metres times `F.fit.h`.

## Style

`macondo` — the engine's graphic figures (`graphic`: flat colours, long limbs) toned by the scene's light, with a
simple face (eyes, brows, nose, mouth — `<id>_mood` shows on it), so faces read across the room.

## Who

- `colonel` — the colonel, seventy-five: tall and bony, short grey hair, a grey moustache, his black suit over a white
  collarless shirt, polished black shoes.
- `wife` — his wife, about seventy: small and bony, grey hair in a knot, a faded dark-blue house dress.

## Things (`<id>_with`, or a look's `parts`)

`umbrella` — the colonel's old black umbrella, furled, held like a cane (the crook in the right hand, the ferrule on
the floor). `moustache` — the colonel's (a look's).

## Motions (Kimodo)

The engine's `stand` and `walk`, and: `sit_upright` — sits upright on a bench (the edge of a bed), the hands resting
in the lap, breathing calmly (a loop).
