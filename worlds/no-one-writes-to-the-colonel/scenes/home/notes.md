# The House

Stage 2 of the game (the look): the colonel's one room on an October Friday at dawn, in the rain — the place that sets
the world's mood (limewash and damp, the zinc roof, the grey light at the door and the window, the fire under the
pot) and its people as they are in the story. No one moves but a look; no camera moves.

- **Where things are.** The viewer stands at the room's open side (camera `picture`). Earth floor at 0 (`floor`, the
  rooster's string and his corn painted on it), the zinc roof at 2.9 (`roof`, a card laid face down), the back wall at
  distance 5.0 (`back_wall`: the window x 1.25…2.25, the soot and the shelf over the stove, the hammock's hooks, a
  calendar), the side walls at across ±3.0 from 1.24 to 5.0 (`left_wall` with the open doorway at distance 3.2 —
  `door`, its frame — and the blue door leaf against the wall nearer us; `right_wall`). Shared limewash and yard mud:
  `lib.js`.
- **In the room**: the stove in the back-left corner (`stove`: the pot, the coffee tin with the knife — `stove#tin`;
  its fire the particles `fire`, its embers the light `stove`, key `fire`; `steam`), the hammock before the back wall
  (a layer stirring), the wall clock at twenty to six (`clock#face`; the pendulum a layer), the iron bed under the
  window (`bed` its side, `bed_top`, `bed_end` the two iron ends as copies), the rooster before them, tied to the bed's
  foot (`rooster#head`; head and tail layers the animate jerks and stirs).
- **Outside**: the yard (`yard`, `yard_side`), the banana plants (copies, their crowns in the wind), the neighbours'
  house beyond the door, the fence beyond the window, the rain (`rain_back`, `rain_side`, key `rain`), the grey sky.
- **Light**: the grey morning in at the door and the window (daylight lights `door_day`, `window_day`), a weak far
  `dawn`, and the stove's warm embers on the left.
- **People** (modules/cast, style `macondo`): the colonel in his black suit before the hammock, the umbrella in his
  hand like a cane, and his wife on the edge of the bed, both looking at the rooster between them. The showcase: she
  looks up at him, he past us; back to the rooster.
