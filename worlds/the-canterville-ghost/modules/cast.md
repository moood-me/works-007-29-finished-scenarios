# cast — the people of “The Canterville Ghost”

A module for scenes with people (elements with `person`: `{"cast": "cast", "who": …}` — people_guide). The parts'
lengths are a man's metres times `F.fit.h`.

## Style

`canterville` — the storybook's round soft figures (`soft`), heads a little smaller, with the world's face
`storybook` (eyes, brows, nose, mouth drawn bold — `<id>_mood` shows on it): faces read across a room.

## Who

- `virginia` — Virginia Otis, fifteen: slight, golden-brown hair loose, a white muslin frock with a pale blue sash.
- `simon` — Sir Simon de Canterville, the ghost: tall, old, a grey-green doublet and a ragged cloak, rusty manacles and
  a chain between them; his hair, beard and ruff glow with his own pale light (`GHOSTLY`, a glowing colour), his eyes
  are red coals (the face `ghost`). A scene gives him a light of his own at `simon#chest`.
- `otis` — Mr Hiram B. Otis, the American Minister: a black frock coat, a grey waistcoat, brown hair going grey.

## Parts

`ruff`, `beard`, `shackles` (manacles and the chain sagging from wrist to wrist) — Sir Simon's; `sash` — Virginia's.

## Motions (Kimodo)

The engine's `stand` and `walk`, and: `clasped` — stands, both hands clasped at the chest, a loop; `spread` — stands
with both arms spread wide to the sides (the ghost's showing himself), a loop; `hands_behind` — stands, hands clasped
behind the back, a loop.
