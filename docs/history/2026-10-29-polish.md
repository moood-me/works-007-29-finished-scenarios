# 2026-10-29 — Polish (`pl`)

Why: Павел: «и польский» — Polish as a further language beside the nine.

- The 29 worlds and the two games (`night-flight`, `the-canterville-ghost`): `"pl"` in `languages`, every world's and
  scene's `lang/pl.json`, each game's `bespoke/lang/pl/strings.json` (937 texts).
- Titles as the Polish editions have them: Dom lalki, Białe noce, W kleszczach lęku, Myszy i ludzie, Kobieta z wydm,
  Stary człowiek i morze, Kwiaty dla Algernona, Kraksa (Dürrenmatt's «Traps»), Gospodarz i parobek (Tolstoy), Liść,
  dzieło Niggle'a, Klara i Słońce, Syrena (Bradbury; also published as «Wołanie we mgle»), Zwyczajny cud, Asia,
  Niewidzialne miasta, Nikt nie pisze do pułkownika, Duma i uprzedzenie, Szklana menażeria, Pogrzebany olbrzym, Ocean
  na końcu drogi, Ci, którzy odchodzą z Omelas, Papierowa menażeria; the games «Nocny lot» and «Duch z Canterville».
- Choices: the player is «ty» — Rivière in masculine forms, who says «pan» to his men; Wirginia in feminine forms,
  «panienka» to sir Szymon. Night Flight keeps its French names; Canterville's are the classic Polish ones (Wirginia,
  sir Szymon, Waszyngton, pani Umney). The library window's prophecy is rhymed anew in Polish, and the game quotes it
  the same way.

How it was checked: moood's `check` (its `pl` branch: `pl` in `i18n/languages.json`), `node packages/check/cli.js <repo> --write`
laid the files out, the translations were written into them, then `check` on the whole repository says «all right».
From the English source, the Russian read beside it as a second view of meaning and tone. Only translations and the
`languages` lists were added; no source text, code or doc was changed.
