# The game's sound recordings

Real recordings from Freesound, licence CC0 (no conditions). Beds (what lasts under a scene) are a piece of ~45 s
cut from the recording, looped (the last 2.5 s mixed into the beginning), the loudness brought to −24 dBFS RMS
(for beds with sharp peaks — `clock`, `typewriter`, `storm`, `inker` — as far as the peaks allow, at most 3 dB of
limiting), MP3 96 kbit/s mono. One-offs (single events) are trimmed tight, the peak brought to about −1 dBFS,
MP3 96 kbit/s mono. The recordings were chosen from Freesound's pages (title, description, tags, length, downloads),
looked at as waveform and spectrogram, and cut with ffmpeg from the sound's HQ preview (MP3 128 kbit/s, which
Freesound serves without login). Most aircraft and the loudest "old" sounds come from craigsmith's digitised
Hollywood effects of the 1930s–60s (optical film tracks): narrow band, some film noise — that is the period sound.
