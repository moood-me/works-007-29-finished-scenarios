# The game's sound recordings

Real recordings from Freesound, licence CC0 (no conditions). Beds (what lasts under a scene) are a piece of ~45 s
cut from the recording (the nightingale ~60 s), looped (the last 2.5 s mixed into the beginning), the loudness
brought to −24 dBFS RMS, MP3 96 kbit/s mono. One-offs (single events) are trimmed tight, the peak brought to −1 dBFS,
MP3 96 kbit/s mono. `storm`, `fire` and `wind` are the same files as in other games of the repository family
(rpg docs/history/2026-10-16-game-sounds.md); the rest were chosen from Freesound's pages (title, description, tags,
length, downloads, rating) and cut with ffmpeg.
